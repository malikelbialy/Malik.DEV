About

I'm Malik El-Bialy, a freelance developer working out of Tanta, Egypt and Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. Most of what I build is commissioned by a real shop, agency, or founder who needs something specific to work — an ordering site for a butcher, a management panel for a Quran teacher, a catalog for a pipe manufacturer.

I work end to end: design, frontend, backend, and the small operational details that make a tool usable day to day — order statuses, coupon systems, branch detection, admin permissions.

Outside client work, I build things I'm curious about — a football stats app, a Minecraft battle royale mod, Islamic and social apps for myself and friends.
